Good to know

Questions, answered.

Quick details about ordering, delivery, prescriptions and store hours.

How do I place an order?

Send your medicine list or a prescription photo on WhatsApp to check availability. The pharmacy will confirm stock with you.

Can I collect my order at the store?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp to confirm availability, then collect your order from Biswas Pharmacy.

Where is free delivery available?

Home delivery is available only within 5 km of Biswas Pharmacy. Orders of ₹100 or more are delivered free; orders below ₹100 have a ₹15 delivery charge.

Can you arrange a medicine that is out of stock?

Send the medicine name on WhatsApp. The pharmacy will check whether it can be arranged and reply to you.

What are the store hours?

Every day, 8:00 AM–1:00 PM and 6:00 PM–10:30 PM.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash and UPI are accepted both at the store and for home-delivery orders.

Can I get my blood pressure or blood sugar checked?

Yes. In-store blood pressure checks cost ₹10 and blood sugar checks cost ₹50.

Do you provide nebulizer service and sell nebulizer machines?

Yes. Nebulizer service is available in-store, and machines are sold subject to availability. Please ask the pharmacy team for the service charge.

Do I need a prescription?

A valid prescription is required for medicines that need one. You can send its photo on WhatsApp to check availability; the pharmacy will confirm the prescription before supplying the medicine.
